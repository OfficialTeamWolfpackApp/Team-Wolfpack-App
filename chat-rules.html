PACK CHAT COMMUNITY RULES
1. BE RESPECTFUL
Treat other Pack members with respect. Personal attacks, bullying, harassment and abusive behaviour are not welcome.
2. NO THREATS OR INTIMIDATION
Do not post threats, intimidation, targeted abuse or content intended to make another person feel unsafe.
3. NO SPAM
Do not flood the chat, repeatedly post the same message, advertise excessively or deliberately disrupt conversations.
4. PROTECT PRIVATE INFORMATION
Do not post private or sensitive information such as addresses, passwords, financial details or another person's personal information.
5. DO NOT IMPERSONATE TEAM WOLFPACK
Do not pretend to be MR WOLFIE, MRS WOLFIE, a Team Wolfpack administrator, fighter or team representative.
6. USE THE REPORT SYSTEM RESPONSIBLY
Use REPORT when a message genuinely breaks the rules. Deliberately false or abusive reporting may result in moderation action.
7. ADMIN MODERATION
Team Wolfpack administrators may review reports, delete messages, mute members or block members where necessary to protect the community.
By entering Pack Chat, you agree to follow these rules while using the community chat.
