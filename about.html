TEAM WOLFPACK

WHO WE ARE

Team Wolfpack is a boxing team built around strength, loyalty, unity and purpose.

Created by Co-Founders Chris “Mr Wolfie” and Zoey “Mrs Wolfie” James , the team brings fighters, corner teams and supporters together under one identity.

The pack is about more than boxing. It is about standing together, supporting one another and giving people a place where they can grow in confidence, discipline and strength.

WHAT WE STAND FOR
