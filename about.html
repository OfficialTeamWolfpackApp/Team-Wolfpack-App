WHO WE ARE

Team Wolfpack is more than just a boxing team. We are a growing family built on strength, loyalty, unity, respect and purpose.

Established in 2025, Team Wolfpack was created to bring people together through a shared passion for boxing, while building something that reaches far beyond the ropes of a boxing ring.

We believe that every fighter deserves encouragement, every team member deserves recognition and every supporter deserves to feel part of something meaningful.

Whether you’re a fighter preparing for your next challenge, a member of the corner team, a sponsor supporting the journey or someone cheering from the sidelines, you are an important part of what makes Team Wolfpack special.

We are united by one belief:

Different journeys. Different challenges. One family. One pack.

01 OUR STORY
BUILT ON STRENGTH. DRIVEN BY PURPOSE.

Team Wolfpack was co-founded by Chris “Mr Wolfie” James and Zoey “Mrs Wolfie” James, with a shared vision of creating a team where people could come together, support one another and work towards something bigger than themselves.

What began as a passion for boxing has grown into a team identity built around determination, respect, commitment and the belief that nobody should have to face their journey alone.

From the early stages of building the team to developing our own boxing apps, fighter profiles, sponsorship opportunities and growing online presence, every step has been about creating something meaningful.

We want Team Wolfpack to represent more than what happens on fight night.

We want it to represent the hard work that happens behind the scenes, the sacrifices made during training, the people who stand in the corners and the supporters who believe in our fighters.

Every achievement, no matter how big or small, is another chapter in our story.

Our journey started in 2025. Our ambition is to keep moving forward.

02 THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE PACK
TWO PEOPLE. ONE SHARED VISION.

At the heart of Team Wolfpack are its co-founders, Chris “Mr Wolfie” James and Zoey “Mrs Wolfie” James.

Together, they share a commitment to supporting fighters, strengthening the team and creating an environment where everyone feels valued.

CHRIS “MR WOLFIE” JAMES — Co-Founder • Team Manager

As Team Manager, Chris is committed to helping Team Wolfpack develop, grow and move forward.

His involvement includes supporting the team, developing opportunities, building relationships with sponsors and supporters, and helping create a stronger identity for Team Wolfpack.

Through the development of the Team Wolfpack App and its growing digital presence, the aim is to make the team more connected, accessible and visible.

Chris believes that building a successful team isn’t just about individual achievements. It’s about creating an environment where people support one another and where every member has a role to play.

ZOEY “MRS WOLFIE” JAMES — Co-Founder • Boxer • Team Wolfie

Zoey represents the fighting spirit of Team Wolfpack.

As a boxer representing Team Wolfie, her journey reflects the determination, discipline and resilience that boxing demands.

Every training session, every challenge and every opportunity to step into the ring is part of her development as a fighter.

Her dedication to boxing is also an important part of the inspiration behind Team Wolfpack.

Through her journey, we want to demonstrate the importance of believing in yourself, working towards your goals and never giving up when challenges arise.

Together, Chris and Zoey are working to build a team that stands for something meaningful.

Two different roles. One shared commitment. One Wolfpack.

03 BUILT THROUGH BOXING
MORE THAN A SPORT. A WAY OF LIFE.

Boxing is at the heart of Team Wolfpack.

But to us, boxing represents far more than punches, rounds and victories.

It represents discipline, courage, resilience, respect and the determination to keep moving forward.

Every boxer has their own journey.

Some are stepping into the ring for the first time. Others are developing their skills, preparing for new challenges or working towards championship opportunities.

Every journey comes with its own sacrifices, setbacks, achievements and lessons.

At Team Wolfpack, we believe that progress deserves recognition.

We recognise the early mornings, demanding training sessions, physical preparation and mental determination required to compete.

We also recognise the importance of the people who stand behind each fighter.

From corner teams providing guidance to family members and supporters offering encouragement, nobody reaches their goals entirely alone.

Our aim is to create a supportive environment where fighters can continue developing, challenging themselves and working towards their ambitions.

We don’t measure strength only by victories. We measure it by the courage to keep going.

04 UNITED AS ONE PACK
DIFFERENT FIGHTERS. ONE IDENTITY.

One of the foundations of Team Wolfpack is the belief that individual fighters and teams can maintain their own identities while remaining united by a common purpose.

Within the wider Wolfpack, we recognise the different journeys represented by Team Wolfie and Team Sloan.

Each fighter brings their own personality, experiences, ambitions and determination.

Each team has its own identity.

But what connects everyone is the commitment to supporting one another and representing the values of Team Wolfpack.

We believe that being part of a team means celebrating each other’s achievements, providing encouragement through difficult moments and recognising the contributions of everyone involved.

From the fighters stepping into the ring to the corner teams standing beside them, every individual plays an important role.

Team Wolfpack is about creating a sense of belonging.

It’s about knowing that when one member faces a challenge, they have people standing behind them.

When one member achieves something special, the entire pack has a reason to celebrate.

We may follow different paths, but we stand together as one pack.

05 OUR SPONSORS & SUPPORTERS
THE PEOPLE WHO HELP MAKE IT POSSIBLE.

Behind every fighter is a network of people who believe in their journey.

At Team Wolfpack, our sponsors and supporters are an important part of everything we’re building.

Their involvement helps us continue developing opportunities, supporting our fighters and strengthening the team.

We recognise that support comes in many different forms.

Some people contribute through business sponsorships.

Others offer personal support, encouragement, time or assistance.

Every contribution matters.

Our aim is to create meaningful relationships with the people and businesses who choose to support Team Wolfpack.

Through our dedicated sponsorship and supporter sections, we want to recognise those who stand behind our fighters and provide opportunities for them to become more involved.

Our Team Wolfpack App also provides a platform where sponsors and supporters can receive recognition and become part of our growing community.

We believe that supporting a fighter should mean more than simply having your name displayed.

It should mean becoming part of their journey, sharing in their progress and knowing that your support is genuinely appreciated.

Our fighters may be the ones stepping into the ring, but our supporters help make the journey possible.

06 CONNECTING THE WOLFPACK
OUR TEAM. OUR COMMUNITY. OUR APP.

Team Wolfpack is about more than the people who can attend a fight night.

We want to bring our entire community together, wherever they may be.

That’s why we’re developing our own digital platforms to make it easier for fighters, team members, sponsors and supporters to stay connected.

THE TEAM WOLFPACK APP

Our official Team Wolfpack App is designed to become the central home of the Wolfpack.

It brings together different parts of the team in one dedicated platform.

Through the app, supporters can explore upcoming fights, discover fighter profiles, follow announcements, view galleries, access ticket information and learn more about the people and businesses supporting our journey.

It also provides a way to showcase our fighters, recognise our team members and keep our growing community informed.

As Team Wolfpack develops, we aim to continue improving the app with new features, information and opportunities for supporters to become involved.

THE MRS WOLFIE BOXING APP

Alongside our main team platform, we also have a dedicated Mrs Wolfie Boxing App.

This gives supporters a separate place to follow Zoey’s boxing journey, including fight information, upcoming events and updates relating to her development as a fighter.

Both apps are part of our commitment to building a stronger connection between the team and the people who support us.

We want Team Wolfpack to be accessible, engaging and connected both inside and outside the boxing community.

One team. One community. Connected through the Wolfpack.

07 OUR FUTURE
THE JOURNEY IS ONLY BEGINNING.

Team Wolfpack was established with a vision, but we believe that what we’re building is only the beginning.

Our ambition is to continue growing into a recognised and respected team within boxing.

We want to support fighters as they develop, help create opportunities for future challenges and strengthen the relationships that make our team possible.

We also want to continue expanding our community, welcoming new supporters and developing our digital platforms.

As Team Wolfpack grows, our commitment will remain the same.

We will continue to value loyalty, respect, determination, unity and the people who stand alongside us.

We understand that building something meaningful takes time.

There will be challenges to overcome, lessons to learn and moments that test our determination.

But those challenges are part of the journey.

Our future isn’t defined by one fight, one result or one individual.

It’s defined by the commitment we share and the progress we make together.

We are not just building a boxing team. We are building something that people can be proud to belong to.
