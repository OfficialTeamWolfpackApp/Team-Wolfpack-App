MANUAL PAYMENT APPROVAL WORKFLOW

1. Application received

Applicant selects a business or supporter package. Status: NEW.

2. Awaiting payment

An administrator contacts the applicant with payment instructions. Status: AWAITING PAYMENT.

3. Payment verified

Only an authorised administrator may record verified payment. No card or bank details are displayed publicly.

4. Membership activated

Trusted backend records approval, tier, start and expiry dates; benefits then become active for 12 months.

Security requirement: Buttons to verify payment and activate memberships will remain unavailable until Firebase authentication, administrator-only database rules and a trusted approval endpoint have been implemented and tested.

MEMBERSHIP RENEWAL ALERTS

Sign in as an administrator to check renewal alerts. No reminders are sent automatically.
