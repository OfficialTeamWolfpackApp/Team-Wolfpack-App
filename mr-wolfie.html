THE STORY

ABOUT MR WOLFIE

Chris “Mr Wolfie” James is the Co-Founder and Joint Owner of Team Wolfpack and the Manager to Team Wolfie.

Working behind the scenes, his role is centred around supporting the team, helping keep everything moving and making sure the people representing Team Wolfpack have someone in their corner.

Together with Zoey “Mrs Wolfie” James , he co-founded Team Wolfpack around the values of strength, loyalty, unity and purpose.

As Manager to Team Wolfie, Mr Wolfie supports the journey inside and outside the ring — helping with preparation, organisation, opportunities and the continued development of the wider Team Wolfpack family.

For Mr Wolfie, the purpose is bigger than one fight or one event. It is about building something where people support one another, grow together and keep moving forward.
